How I approach architecture

  • Secure by design

    Security controls belong in the architecture, not bolted on after deployment.

  • Automate the repeatable

    Infrastructure should be reproducible, version-controlled and deployed through code.

  • Identity is the control plane

    Modern security starts with identity, privilege, device trust and continuous evaluation.

  • Architecture must ship

    A design has to survive implementation, operations, budgets and real-world constraints.

  • AI with guardrails

    AI adoption needs governed data, controlled identities, security boundaries and observability.

  • Evidence over slideware

    Decisions are validated through proofs of concept, testing and measurable outcomes.
